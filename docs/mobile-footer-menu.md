# Mobile footer and navigation — published update

Phone widths up to 767px use the existing Alphastar colors and content with a full-width brand introduction, side-by-side Quick Links / Our Services columns, and full-width contact details below. Headings, line heights, link sizes and spacing are adjusted only inside that media query. English and Tagalog labels wrap within the columns. The existing contact links, clinic hours, social destinations and legal links are retained.

The phone navigation opens as a right-hand drawer with a Menu header, close button, dimmed backdrop and transform transition. It is moved outside the transformed fixed navbar only on phones, so it stays attached to the viewport even when the topbar is hidden. Links retain their existing destinations and colors. The page behind is inert and scroll-blocked; Tab stays inside. Close, Escape, backdrop tap and navigation links dismiss it. Resizing restores the existing tablet/desktop placement, and reduced-motion preferences skip the animation. Reopening cancels an old close timer.

`node test/mobile-chrome-layout.cjs` compares tablet/desktop geometry against the committed baseline at 768/1024/1440px and tests both languages at six phone sizes, including short landscape. It verifies unchanged footer colors, wrapping, drawer positioning, focus/scroll containment, dismissal, navigation, resize and reduced motion. Fixtures make no external requests, sign-ins or real chats. Screenshots are saved in `test-results/mobile-chrome/`; header/chat controls are hidden only for the footer screenshot, not in the actual page.
